## Profile Overview

### Purpose
The Foundational Profile provides the minimum viable set of controls necessary to establish basic AI governance. It is designed for organizations starting their AI governance journey, deploying low-risk AI systems, or with limited resources.

### Scope
- **Target Organizations**: Small organizations, startups, pilot AI programs
- **AI Systems**: Low-risk, internal productivity tools, non-critical applications
- **Implementation Timeline**: 3-6 months with part-time team
- **Resource Requirements**: Minimal (part-time governance team, basic documentation)

### Key Characteristics
- Essential governance foundations
- Basic risk awareness and documentation
- Minimal compliance requirements
- Simple monitoring and oversight

## Implementation Guidance

### Prerequisites
- Executive sponsorship for AI governance program
- Basic understanding of organizational AI systems
- Commitment to documentation and basic policies

### Phase 1: Foundation (Months 1-2)
**Focus**: Establish basic governance structure

**Priority Controls**:
1. GOV-2.1: Assign AI accountability (identify AI owner)
2. GOV-1.2: Document AI systems and purposes
3. MAP-1.1: Characterize AI system purposes
4. GOV-2.3: Establish executive oversight (monthly briefings)
5. GOV-6.1: Allocate basic resources

**Deliverables**: AI governance charter, role assignments, AI system inventory

### Phase 2: Risk Management (Months 2-4)
**Focus**: Identify and categorize AI risks

**Priority Controls**:
1. MAP-1.5: Define risk tolerance for AI
2. MAP-2.1: Categorize AI tasks and methods
3. MAP-5.1: Basic impact assessment
4. MAN-1.1: Establish go/no-go criteria
5. MAN-1.2: Prioritize identified risks

**Deliverables**: Risk register, categorization scheme, decision framework

### Phase 3: Monitoring & Improvement (Months 4-6)
**Focus**: Implement basic monitoring and feedback

**Priority Controls**:
1. MEA-2.4: Production monitoring setup
2. MAN-4.3: Incident reporting process
3. GOV-4.2: Stakeholder feedback channels
4. MEA-4.3: Performance trend tracking
5. GOV-1.4: Compliance monitoring (basic checklists)

**Deliverables**: Monitoring dashboards, incident log, feedback process

## Common Challenges

**Challenge 1: Limited Resources**
- **Solution**: Focus on documentation before automation; use spreadsheets and basic tools initially

**Challenge 2: Unclear AI System Inventory**
- **Solution**: Start with obvious AI systems; expand inventory iteratively

**Challenge 3: Lack of AI Expertise**
- **Solution**: Leverage online resources, NIST AI RMF guidance; consider external training

**Challenge 4: Resistance to Governance Overhead**
- **Solution**: Emphasize risk reduction and business value; start with lightweight processes

## Advancement Path

Once Foundational Profile is mature (6-12 months), consider advancing to:

**Next Step**: **Balanced Profile**
- Adds 15 additional controls (~50 total)
- Increases rigor to B2 for existing controls
- Introduces systematic programs (vs ad-hoc approaches)
- Suitable when: Resources increase, AI systems become more critical, regulatory requirements emerge

**Timeline**: Allow 6-12 months in Foundational before advancing
**Prerequisites for Advancement**: Maturity Level 2-3 achieved on foundational controls
