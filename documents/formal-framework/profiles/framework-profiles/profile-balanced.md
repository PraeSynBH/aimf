## Profile Overview

### Purpose
The Balanced Profile represents a comprehensive, enterprise-grade AI governance program suitable for most moderate-risk AI deployments. It balances thoroughness with practicality, providing systematic governance without requiring maximum rigor.

### Scope
- **Target Organizations**: Mid-market to large enterprises, mature startups
- **AI Systems**: Moderate-risk, customer-facing, business-critical applications
- **Implementation Timeline**: 6-12 months with dedicated team
- **Resource Requirements**: Moderate (dedicated governance team, formal processes, tooling)

### Key Characteristics
- Comprehensive governance across all functions
- Systematic programs (not ad-hoc)
- Formal documentation and validation
- Regular audits and reviews
- Stakeholder engagement

## Implementation Guidance

### Prerequisites
- Foundational Profile complete OR mature governance program
- Dedicated AI governance team (2-4 FTE)
- Executive commitment to systematic programs
- Budget for governance tools and training

### Phase 1: Governance Enhancement (Months 1-3)
**Focus**: Elevate governance from basic to systematic

**Priority Controls**:
1. GOV-1.3: Integrate AI risk into enterprise risk framework
2. GOV-1.4: Systematic compliance monitoring (KPIs, audits)
3. GOV-3.1: Establish interdisciplinary teams
4. GOV-4.1: Systematic risk culture program
5. GOV-6.2: Capability building investments

**Deliverables**: Integrated risk framework, audit program, diversity metrics

### Phase 2: Comprehensive Assessment (Months 3-6)
**Focus**: Thorough risk and impact assessment

**Priority Controls**:
1. MAP-1.6: Comprehensive requirements (socio-technical)
2. MAP-2.3: Scientific TEVV planning
3. MAP-3.1/3.2: Benefits/costs analysis (quantified)
4. MAP-5.1: Comprehensive impact assessment
5. MAP-5.2: Systematic stakeholder engagement

**Deliverables**: Requirements documentation, TEVV plan, impact reports

### Phase 3: Robust Measurement (Months 6-9)
**Focus**: Establish validated measurement programs

**Priority Controls**:
1. MEA-1.1: Comprehensive measurement framework
2. MEA-1.3: Independent evaluation program
3. MEA-2.6/2.7: Safety and security evaluation
4. MEA-2.10/2.11: Privacy and fairness testing
5. MEA-4.2: Expert validation

**Deliverables**: Measurement framework, assessment reports, validation documentation

### Phase 4: Continuous Management (Months 9-12)
**Focus**: Ongoing risk management and improvement

**Priority Controls**:
1. MAN-2.2: Value sustainment program
2. MAN-3.1: Third-party monitoring
3. MAN-4.1: Post-deployment monitoring
4. MAN-4.2: Continuous improvement integration
5. All MANAGE controls at B2

**Deliverables**: Monitoring dashboards, improvement program, sustainment plan

## baseline 2 Implementation Standards

**Key B2 Characteristics**:
- **Formal Documentation**: All processes documented, version-controlled
- **Regular Reviews**: Quarterly governance reviews, annual audits
- **Stakeholder Input**: Systematic engagement with feedback integration
- **Metrics & KPIs**: Defined effectiveness measures for governance
- **Independent Validation**: External or independent internal review
- **Training Programs**: Role-based training with competency assessment

---

## Common Challenges

**Challenge 1: Resource Constraints**
- **Solution**: Phased implementation over 12 months; prioritize high-risk systems first

**Challenge 2: Complexity of Comprehensive Assessment**
- **Solution**: Use external consultants for initial impact assessments; build internal capability over time

**Challenge 3: Stakeholder Engagement Fatigue**
- **Solution**: Streamline feedback mechanisms; use surveys and representative councils

**Challenge 4: Measurement Framework Design**
- **Solution**: Leverage NIST AI RMF metrics; customize incrementally based on data

## Success Criteria

**Profile Implementation Complete When**:
- ✅ 50+ controls implemented at B2 rigor (80%+ of profile)
- ✅ Systematic programs in place (not ad-hoc processes)
- ✅ Independent assessment conducted
- ✅ Stakeholder engagement documented and effective
- ✅ Governance metrics tracked and reported quarterly
- ✅ External audit or review completed successfully

## Advancement Path

**Current Level**: Balanced Profile (Maturity Level 3-4 target)

**Next Step**: **High-Assurance Profile** OR **Industry-Specific Profile**
- High-Assurance: If deploying high-risk/autonomous AI
- Industry Profile: If subject to sector-specific regulations (Financial, Healthcare, Government)

**Timeline**: 12-18 months in Balanced before considering advancement
**Prerequisites**: Maturity Level 3-4 on most controls, regulatory driver for advancement
