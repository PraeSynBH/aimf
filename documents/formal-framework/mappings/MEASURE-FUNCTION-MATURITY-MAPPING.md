## Overview

The MEASURE function employs quantitative, qualitative, or mixed-method tools, techniques, and methodologies to analyze, assess, benchmark, and monitor AI risks and trustworthiness characteristics.

**ISO 42001 Primary Mapping**:
- MEASURE aligns primarily with ISO 42001 **Clause 9 (Performance Evaluation)**, specifically:
  - 9.1 Monitoring, measurement, analysis and evaluation
  - 9.2 Internal audit
  - 9.3 Management review
- Also connects to **Clause 8.2** (AI system impact assessment)
- And **Clause 8.3** (Data for AI system)

**MEASURE Function Structure**:
- **4 Categories**: Methods/Metrics, Trustworthy Characteristics Evaluation, Risk Tracking, Measurement Feedback
- **17 Subcategories**: Comprehensive measurement and evaluation requirements
- **Cross-cutting**: Informed by MAP, feeds MANAGE

## Level 1: Initial - No Systematic AI Measurement

**ISO 42001 Reference**: Gaps in Clause 9 - No systematic monitoring, measurement, or evaluation of AI systems

### Overview
Organizations at Level 1 have no structured approach to measuring AI system performance, risks, or trustworthiness characteristics. AI systems may be deployed without proper testing, validation, or ongoing monitoring.

---

### MEASURE 1: Appropriate Methods and Metrics

#### MEASURE 1.1: Approaches and Metrics for AI Risks
- **Status**: Not selected or implemented
- **Characteristics**:
  - No systematic approach to selecting measurement methods
  - AI risks not quantified or tracked
  - Metrics not defined for AI systems
  - Most significant risks not prioritized for measurement
  - Unmeasured risks not documented
  - No rationale for what is/isn't measured

#### MEASURE 1.2: Appropriateness and Effectiveness of Metrics
- **Status**: Not assessed
- **Characteristics**:
  - Metrics not reviewed for appropriateness
