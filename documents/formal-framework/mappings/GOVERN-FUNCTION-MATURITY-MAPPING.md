## Mapping Approach

The GOVERN function maps across all four capability dimensions but primarily relates to:
1. **AI Governance & Risk Management** (primary)
2. **Organizational Culture & Change** (significant)
3. **Data Governance & Management** (supporting)
4. **Technical Operations (MLOps)** (supporting)

## GOVERN 1: Policies, Processes, Procedures, and Practices

### Level 1: Initial - Ad-Hoc AI Usage

**ISO 42001 Reference**: No AI Management System (AIMS) established (Clause 4-7 gaps)

#### GOVERN 1.1: Legal and Regulatory Requirements
- **Status**: Not understood or documented
- **Characteristics**:
  - No awareness of AI-specific legal requirements
  - No tracking of regulatory changes
  - No compliance documentation
  - No legal review process for AI systems

#### GOVERN 1.2: Trustworthy AI Characteristics Integration
- **Status**: Not integrated into organizational policies
- **Characteristics**:
  - No AI-specific policies exist
  - Trustworthy AI characteristics (valid, reliable, safe, secure, transparent, explainable, privacy-enhanced, fair) not defined
  - No organizational commitment to trustworthy AI

#### GOVERN 1.3: Risk Management Level Determination
- **Status**: No risk management processes
- **Characteristics**:
  - No defined risk tolerance
  - No risk assessment criteria
  - No process to determine appropriate level of risk management
  - Ad-hoc decision-making

#### GOVERN 1.4: Risk Management Process Transparency
- **Status**: No established risk management process
- **Characteristics**:
  - No documented procedures
  - No transparent policies
  - No risk prioritization framework
  - No controls based on risk priorities

#### GOVERN 1.5: Ongoing Monitoring and Review
- **Status**: No monitoring or review processes
- **Characteristics**:
  - No periodic review schedule
  - Roles and responsibilities undefined
  - No monitoring of risk management effectiveness
  - No update mechanisms

#### GOVERN 1.6: AI System Inventory
- **Status**: No inventory mechanism
- **Characteristics**:
  - Unknown number of AI systems in use
  - Shadow AI prevalent
  - No tracking of AI deployments
  - No resource allocation based on risk priorities

#### GOVERN 1.7: Decommissioning and Phase-Out
- **Status**: No decommissioning processes
- **Characteristics**:
  - No safe shutdown procedures
  - No plans for system retirement
  - No consideration of risks during phase-out
  - Systems may be abandoned without proper management
