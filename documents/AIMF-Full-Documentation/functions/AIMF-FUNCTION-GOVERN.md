## Function Overview

The **GOVERN** function establishes the organizational structures, policies, processes, and culture necessary for effective AI risk management. It is the foundational function that enables and shapes all other AI risk management activities.

### Purpose

The GOVERN function cultivates and implements a culture of risk management within organizations developing, deploying, or using AI systems. It ensures that:

- Legal and regulatory requirements are understood and addressed
- Accountability structures are established and maintained
- Workforce capabilities support responsible AI practices
- Organizational culture promotes AI risk awareness
- Stakeholder engagement informs governance decisions
- Resources are appropriately allocated

## Control Details

### GOV-1.1: Legal/Regulatory Compliance Policies

**Purpose**: Ensure organizational awareness of and compliance with applicable AI laws, regulations, guidance, standards, and norms.

**Control Objective**: Policies are established that recognize and address all relevant legal and regulatory requirements for AI systems.

#### Implementation by Tier

**Baseline 1 (Initial)**:
- Awareness of key AI regulations applicable to the organization
- Basic compliance documentation identifying primary regulatory requirements
- Designated responsibility for regulatory awareness (may be part of existing legal/compliance function)

**Baseline 2 (Mature)**:
- Formal policy document addressing all applicable regulations
- Regular legal review process (at least annually)
- Documented compliance assessment for each AI system
- Integration with enterprise compliance framework
- Training on regulatory requirements for AI personnel

**Baseline 3 (High-Risk)**:
- Comprehensive legal framework with continuous monitoring
- External legal counsel engagement for AI-specific matters
- Regulatory change management process with impact assessment
- Proactive engagement with regulators
- Public reporting on regulatory compliance status

#### Evidence Examples

- Written AI policy or policy section addressing legal/regulatory requirements
- Regulatory inventory mapping applicable laws to AI systems
- Compliance assessment documentation
- Legal review records
- Regulatory training completion records

#### Related Controls

- GOV-1.3 (AI Lifecycle Governance)
- GOV-1.4 (Compliance Verification)
- MAP-1.1 (AI System Purpose Characterization)

---

### GOV-1.2: AI Use Scope Definition

**Purpose**: Clearly define and differentiate AI system purposes, uses, and associated risks.

**Control Objective**: Policies establish clear categorization and documentation of all AI system purposes and uses with associated risk levels.

#### Implementation by Tier

**Baseline 1 (Initial)**:
- Basic documentation of AI system purposes
- General awareness of AI use cases in the organization
- High-level categorization of AI systems

**Baseline 2 (Mature)**:
- Formal categorization of all AI systems by purpose, use case, and risk level
- Documented approval process for new AI systems
- AI system registry maintained and updated
- Clear boundaries defined for acceptable AI uses
- Risk-based categorization framework applied consistently

**Baseline 3 (High-Risk)**:
- Comprehensive AI system registry with detailed risk ratings
- Board-level approval required for high-risk systems
- Regular review and updates to system categorizations
- Public transparency on AI system purposes (where appropriate)
- Automated registry management with change tracking

#### Evidence Examples

- AI system inventory/registry
- Categorization framework documentation
- Approval workflow documentation
- Risk classification methodology
- Board/leadership approval records for high-risk systems

---

### GOV-1.3: AI Lifecycle Governance

**Purpose**: Integrate AI risk management into the broader organizational risk management framework.

**Control Objective**: AI risk management is fully integrated with enterprise risk management, with defined risk appetite and tolerance levels.

#### Implementation by Tier

**Baseline 1 (Initial)**:
- AI risks acknowledged in risk framework
- Basic escalation procedures for AI-related risks
- AI risks discussed in existing risk forums

**Baseline 2 (Mature)**:
- AI risk management fully integrated with enterprise risk framework
- Defined risk appetite statements for AI
- AI risks included in enterprise risk reporting
- Clear escalation paths and decision authorities
- Regular risk review cycles for AI systems

**Baseline 3 (High-Risk)**:
- Quantitative AI risk thresholds defined and monitored
- Automated risk monitoring with real-time alerts
- Real-time risk dashboards for leadership
- Independent risk validation
- AI risk integrated into strategic planning

#### Evidence Examples

- Enterprise risk management framework (showing AI integration)
- Risk appetite/tolerance statements for AI
- Risk reporting samples including AI risks
- Escalation procedure documentation
- Risk dashboard screenshots

---

### GOV-2.1: AI Accountability Assignment

**Purpose**: Establish clear roles, responsibilities, and authorities for AI risk management.

**Control Objective**: All AI risk management responsibilities are formally assigned with clear accountability.

#### Implementation by Tier

**Baseline 1 (Initial)**:
- Key AI responsibilities identified
- Informal assignments to existing personnel
- Basic understanding of who handles AI-related matters

**Baseline 2 (Mature)**:
- Formal RACI matrix for AI governance
- Documented role descriptions for AI responsibilities
- Clear escalation paths documented
- Dedicated AI governance personnel (may be part-time)
- Regular review of role effectiveness

**Baseline 3 (High-Risk)**:
- Comprehensive organizational structure for AI governance
- Independent AI oversight function
- Board-level accountability established
- External advisory mechanisms
- Personal accountability documented for senior leaders

#### Evidence Examples

- RACI matrix for AI governance
- Role descriptions/job descriptions
- Organizational chart showing AI governance structure
- Board committee charter (for B3)
- Advisory board terms of reference (for B3)

---

### GOV-2.2: AI Competency Development

**Purpose**: Build and maintain workforce capabilities for AI risk and governance.

**Control Objective**: Training and awareness programs ensure personnel have necessary AI risk and governance competencies.

#### Implementation by Tier

**Baseline 1 (Initial)**:
- Basic AI awareness training available
- Foundational understanding of AI risks for relevant personnel
- General introduction to responsible AI practices

**Baseline 2 (Mature)**:
- Role-based training programs
- Competency assessments for AI personnel
- Regular refresher training
- Specialized training for AI developers
- Training effectiveness measured

**Baseline 3 (High-Risk)**:
- Comprehensive certification programs
- Continuous professional development requirements
- Specialized expertise development
- External training validation
- Industry-recognized certifications required for key roles

#### Evidence Examples

- Training curriculum/program documentation
- Training completion records
- Competency assessment results
- Certification records
- Training effectiveness metrics

## Implementation Guidance

### Getting Started with GOVERN

1. **Assess Current State**: Evaluate existing governance structures, policies, and capabilities
2. **Identify Gaps**: Compare current state to target tier requirements
3. **Prioritize Actions**: Focus on foundational controls (GOV-1.1, GOV-1.2, GOV-2.1)
4. **Build Foundation**: Establish basic policies and accountability before advancing to more sophisticated controls
5. **Iterate**: Continuously improve governance practices based on experience and feedback

### Common Implementation Challenges

| Challenge | Mitigation |
|-----------|------------|
| Lack of executive sponsorship | Start with awareness campaign; quantify risks and opportunities |
| Unclear accountability | Begin with informal assignments; formalize based on organizational structure |
| Resource constraints | Integrate with existing governance functions; prioritize high-risk areas |
| Rapidly evolving regulations | Establish monitoring process; engage external advisors |
| Cultural resistance | Focus on benefits; celebrate successes; integrate with existing culture |

### Integration with Other Functions

The GOVERN function informs and enables all other functions:

- **MAP**: Governance policies define risk tolerance and acceptable AI uses
- **MEASURE**: Governance determines measurement requirements and standards
- **MANAGE**: Governance establishes authorities and processes for risk response

## Assessment Criteria

### Scoring Methodology

Each GOVERN control is assessed on the canonical **0-4 point scale / ML1-ML5** — the same instrument defined in `implementation/AIMF-ASSESSMENT-GUIDE.md`:

| Score | Maturity Level | Description |
|-------|----------------|-------------|
| 0 | ML1 - Initial | No governance capability, or ad-hoc and individual-dependent with no documentation |
| 1 | ML2 - Developing | Governance documented but not consistently applied |
| 2 | ML3 - Defined | Documented and consistently applied organization-wide (>90%), with training and audit |
| 3 | ML4 - Managed | All of ML3 plus quantitative metrics on governance effectiveness, reviewed and used in decisions |
| 4 | ML5 - Optimizing | All of ML4 plus systematic continuous improvement of governance practice |

The retired 0-3 lens scored a GOVERN control no higher than "Established" (ML3 = 2 points); ML4 and ML5 were unreachable under it. The `0-3` → `0-4` conversion is tabulated in `implementation/AIMF-ASSESSMENT-GUIDE.md`.

### Baseline Alignment

| Score | Maturity Level | Baseline Alignment |
|-------|----------------|--------------------|
| 0 | ML1 - Initial | Below B1 requirements; remediation needed |
| 1 | ML2 - Developing | Approaching B1; not yet sufficient for B2 |
| 2 | ML3 - Defined | Appropriate for B1 and B2 |
| 3 | ML4 - Managed | Appropriate for B2; developing toward B3 |
| 4 | ML5 - Optimizing | Appropriate for B3 |
