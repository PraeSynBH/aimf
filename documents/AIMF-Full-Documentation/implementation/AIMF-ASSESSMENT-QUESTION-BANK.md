## What this document is

This is the instrument behind the AIMF assessment: **one row per control, for all 72
controls of the catalog**, each row carrying the question to ask, the evidence that answers
it, the role to ask, the audience that owns the evidence, and five behavioural anchors that
fix what a 0, 1, 2, 3 and 4 answer looks like for that control.

It exists so that two assessors, given the same evidence, produce the same number. A
question on its own says what to look for; the anchors say **what counts as which score**.
Without them the scale is applied from recollection, and two engagements produce numbers
that cannot be compared — which is the whole promise of a fixed-price assessment.

`AIMF-ASSESSMENT-GUIDE.md` carries the same 72 questions in compact form (control,
question, evidence, role) for use inside an interview; the anchors live here. Both are
rendered from one source, so they cannot disagree.

## The scoring scale

Every control is scored on the framework's single five-level CMMI-based scale, written as
**0 to 4 points mapped to ML1-ML5**. ML3 (Defined) scores 2 and is the standard
"implemented" level; ML1 scores 0 so that ad-hoc practice is not rewarded.

| Score | Maturity level | What the level means | What the answer must show |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | ML1 - Initial | Capability absent or undocumented; holds only in individuals' heads. | The artefact does not exist and the answer is a description of intent. |
| 1 | ML2 - Developing | Documented but inconsistent; partial coverage. | The artefact exists but is partial, unapproved, stale, or applied to some teams or systems only. |
| 2 | ML3 - Defined | Documented, approved, applied organization-wide and audited. | The artefact exists, is current and approved, and covers every in-scope AI system. |
| 3 | ML4 - Managed | ML3 plus quantitative measures reviewed against targets and used in decisions. | The level-2 evidence plus a measured indicator of whether the control works, reviewed on a cadence. |
| 4 | ML5 - Optimizing | ML4 plus systematic, repeated improvement driven by that measurement. | The level-3 evidence plus a documented change traceable to the measurement, repeated over at least two review cycles. |
<!-- END GENERATED:bank-scale -->

**How the anchors are built.** Anchor 0 describes absence. Anchor 1 describes a partial
artefact. Anchor 2 describes the artefact in force organization-wide — the boundary a policy
alone will not cross. Anchor 3 adds a **measurement** of whether the control works. Anchor 4
adds **improvement driven by that measurement**, repeated rather than one-off. The ladder is
monotone by construction and enforced that way: every anchor 3 begins "Level 2 evidence
plus…" and every anchor 4 begins "Level 3 evidence plus…", so a score can only be awarded
if the evidence for the level below is present. `scripts/aimf_question_bank.py check` fails
if that shape is broken.

**Scoring rules that the anchors depend on.**

1. **Score the organization, not the best team.** The anchor is met when it holds across
   all in-scope AI systems, or the answer must say which systems it does not cover — and
   that is a level-1 answer.
2. **A document is not application.** A policy, procedure or register that exists but is
   not followed scores 1, not 2. Ask for the artefact **and** for evidence of use.
3. **Metrics must be used.** A dashboard that nobody reviews, or a metric with no target
   and no decision attached, does not reach score 3.
4. **One improvement is not a system.** A single remediation reaches score 3 at most;
   score 4 requires the pattern across at least two review cycles.
5. **Unobtainable evidence is not a zero.** If the artefact cannot be produced in the
   session, record the control as unresolved and re-book it. Absence from the interview is
   not absence of the control, and silently scoring it 0 is how a reproducible instrument
   starts producing irreproducible numbers.

## How a row is read

| Field | What it fixes |
|---|---|
| `Control` | The catalog `Control_ID`, spelled exactly as `core/AIMF-CONTROL-CATALOG.csv` spells it. A score is only citable against this ID. |
| `Question` | The single question the assessor asks. Ask it as written; follow-ups are allowed, rewordings that change the subject are not. |
| `Evidence` | The artefacts that answer the question. Anything scored above 0 must be shown, not described. |
| `Role` | The role that is asked. The same evidence may be volunteered by others, but this is the role accountable for producing it. |
| `Audience` | Leadership, Technical, Governance or Change Agent — derived from the catalog dimension, not chosen per question (see below). |
| `Anchor 0` … `Anchor 4` | What the answer looks like at each score, in that order, on the 0-4 / ML1-ML5 scale. |

## Maintenance

Both this document and the question tables in `AIMF-ASSESSMENT-GUIDE.md` are **generated**.
Do not hand-edit a generated region: it will be overwritten, and `check` will report drift
until it is.

- **Authored source**: `implementation/AIMF-ASSESSMENT-QUESTION-BANK.csv` — question,
  evidence, role and the five anchors per control.
- **Control set, function and dimension**: `core/AIMF-CONTROL-CATALOG.csv`.
- **Render and validate**: `python3 scripts/aimf_question_bank.py generate` rewrites the
  generated regions; `check` fails on a missing or unknown control, an empty question,
  evidence, role or anchor, a repeated anchor, a broken anchor ladder, a coverage count that
  does not match the catalog, or a document that has drifted from the source.
  `python3 scripts/aimf_mappings.py check` runs the same validation as part of the mapping
  drift check, so a bank edit cannot ship without the coverage and anchor checks passing.

**What this instrument does not yet claim.** Coverage, role assignment and anchors are
complete for all 72 controls. Inter-rater reliability has not been measured: no blind
double-scoring run has been recorded against this bank yet, so the anchors are a
well-specified instrument rather than a validated psychometric one. An assessment report
must state that, and must carry the AIMF version that was scored.
