## Profile Overview

### Purpose
The Government Profile addresses requirements for federal, state, and local government agencies deploying AI in public services, administrative functions, and public safety. Emphasizes transparency, stakeholder engagement, civil rights protections, and public accountability.

### Scope
- **Target Organizations**: Federal, state, local government agencies
- **AI Systems**: Public-facing services, administrative decisions, benefits administration, public safety, infrastructure management
- **Regulatory Drivers**: Executive Orders 14110 & 14179, OMB guidance, civil rights laws, state AI laws, FOIA
- **Implementation Timeline**: 12-18 months with governance + legal + public engagement teams

## Key Regulatory Requirements

### Federal Mandates
- **Executive Order 14110**: Safe, Secure, and Trustworthy AI (October 2023)
  - High-risk AI reporting to OMB
  - Civil rights impact assessments
  - Public transparency requirements
- **Executive Order 14179**: Advancing AI Innovation in Government
- **OMB Guidance**: M-21-06 (Evidence-Based Policymaking), future AI-specific guidance
- **FOIA**: Freedom of Information Act - AI algorithm transparency
- **Civil Rights Laws**: Title VI, Section 504, ADA - non-discrimination

### State Laws
- **Colorado AI Act**: Deployer requirements for high-risk AI
- **California AB 302, AB 331**: State agency AI inventory and impact assessments
- **Other state laws**: Varying transparency and accountability requirements

## Industry-Specific Control Emphases

### Transparency & Public Accountability (PARAMOUNT)
**Controls**: MEA-2.8 (Transparency Evaluation), GOV-4.3 (Transparency Communication), MEA-2.9 (Explainability)
**B2-B3 Requirements**:
- Public AI system inventory (updated regularly)
- Plain-language explanations for citizens
- AI decision explanations available upon request
- Public reporting on AI governance (annual minimum)
- FOIA compliance for AI algorithms

### Stakeholder Engagement & Public Input
**Controls**: GOV-4.2 (Stakeholder Feedback), MAP-5.2 (Stakeholder Engagement), MEA-3.3 (User Feedback)
**B2-B3 Requirements**:
- Public comment periods for major AI deployments
- Community input in AI design (especially for vulnerable populations)
- Appeal processes for AI-driven decisions
- External advisory councils (civil rights, community representatives)
- Regular public engagement (town halls, surveys, etc.)

### Civil Rights & Fairness
**Controls**: MEA-2.11 (Fairness Assessment), GOV-3.1 (Interdisciplinary Teams), GOV-3.2 (Demographic Diversity)
**B2-B3 Requirements**:
- Civil rights impact assessments (before deployment)
- Fairness testing across protected classes and vulnerable populations
- Disparate impact analysis
- Regular civil rights audits
- Independent civil rights review

### Accountability & Governance
**Controls**: GOV-2.1 (Accountability Assignment), GOV-2.3 (Executive Oversight), GOV-1.4 (Compliance Monitoring)
**B2-B3 Requirements**:
- Clear public accountability (named officials responsible)
- Legislative reporting requirements
- Inspector General or Government Accountability Office oversight
- Compliance with agency-specific AI policies
- Regular audits and public audit reports

### Public Safety Considerations
**Controls**: MEA-2.6 (Safety Evaluation), MAP-5.1 (Impact Assessment), MAN-2.4 (System Disengage)
**B2-B3 Requirements**:
- Public safety impact assessments
- Emergency override capabilities (human supersede)
- Incident response with public notification
- Safety evaluation by independent experts

## Implementation Phases

### Phase 1: Transparency Foundation (Months 1-4)
1. AI system inventory (public)
2. Public engagement strategy
3. Transparency policies and procedures
4. Initial civil rights impact assessment

### Phase 2: Comprehensive Assessment (Months 4-10)
1. Impact assessments for all AI systems
2. Fairness and civil rights testing
3. Stakeholder engagement program
4. Appeal and feedback mechanisms

### Phase 3: Public Accountability (Months 10-18)
1. Public reporting (first annual report)
2. External advisory council
3. Legislative and oversight engagement
4. Continuous monitoring with public dashboard

## Executive Order 14110 Compliance

**High-Risk AI Requirements** (if applicable):
- Report high-risk AI systems to OMB
- Conduct impact assessments
- Implement fairness and bias testing
- Establish human review processes
- Public transparency on high-risk systems

**Profile Support**: AIMF Government Profile aligns with all EO 14110 requirements

## Unique Government Considerations

### FOIA & Algorithm Transparency
- Policies for responding to FOIA requests about AI algorithms
- Balancing transparency with security/proprietary concerns
- Public access to AI decision logic (to extent feasible)

### Legislative Oversight
- Regular reporting to legislative bodies
- Testimony preparation for AI-related hearings
- Budget justification for AI governance programs

### Civil Service Considerations
- Training for government employees on AI
- Procurement processes for AI vendors
- Workforce transition as AI automates tasks
