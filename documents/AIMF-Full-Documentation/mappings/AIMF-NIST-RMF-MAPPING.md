## Framework Overview

The NIST AI Risk Management Framework provides a structured approach to managing risks associated with artificial intelligence. It is voluntary, rights-preserving, non-sector specific, and use-case agnostic.

### Core Functions

1. **GOVERN**: Cultivate the organization's culture and context for trustworthy AI
2. **MAP**: Establish context to frame AI risks
3. **MEASURE**: Employ methods to analyze, assess, and track AI risks
4. **MANAGE**: Allocate resources to identified risks on a regular basis

### Trustworthy AI Characteristics

- **Valid and Reliable**: Systems perform consistently and accurately
- **Safe**: Do not pose unreasonable safety risks
- **Secure and Resilient**: Robust against threats and adaptable
- **Accountable and Transparent**: Clear governance and explainable
- **Explainable and Interpretable**: Decisions can be understood
- **Privacy-Enhanced**: Protect individual privacy
- **Fair - with Harmful Bias Managed**: Equitable and minimize bias

## Integration with Other Frameworks

### NIST AI RMF ↔ ISO 42001
- GOVERN function aligns with ISO 42001 Clauses 4-7
- MAP function aligns with ISO 42001 Clause 6 (Planning)
- MEASURE function aligns with ISO 42001 Clause 9 (Performance Evaluation)
- MANAGE function aligns with ISO 42001 Clause 8 (Operation) and 10 (Improvement)
