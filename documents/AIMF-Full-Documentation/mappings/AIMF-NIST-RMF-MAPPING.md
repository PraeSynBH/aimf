## How to read this mapping

The NIST AI RMF describes *what* an organisation should be able to do (functions, categories, subcategories). The AIMF describes *how far* an organisation has got (the five maturity levels) and *who* owns it (the four capability dimensions). The two are mapped here control by control:

| NIST AI RMF | AIMF | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| Function (GOVERN, MAP, MEASURE, MANAGE) | Catalog `Function` | one AIMF control sits in exactly one function |
| Subcategory (e.g. `GOVERN 1.1`) | Catalog `NIST_RMF_Subcategory` | one-to-one: each AIMF control satisfies exactly one subcategory |
| Category outcome | Catalog `Category` | the AIMF category states the governance outcome the subcategory asks for |
| Framework "tiers" (partial to adaptive risk management) | `Maturity_Level_Min` to `Maturity_Level_Target` (levels 1-5) | AIMF levels are a five-step refinement of the RMF's four tiers |

Every row below is generated. Identifier vocabulary follows the catalog (`GOVERN 1.1`), not the RMF's internal `GV-1.1` short form, so that cross-references between AIMF documents resolve.

## NIST AI RMF tiers and AIMF maturity levels

| AI RMF tier | AIMF level | What it looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 - Partial | Level 1 Initial | Risk management is ad hoc; the function's subcategories are unmet or undocumented |
| Tier 2 - Risk-informed | Level 2 Developing | The function's subcategories are met for in-scope systems with partial evidence |
| Tier 3 - Repeatable | Level 3 Defined | The subcategories are met systematically, with documented evidence and named owners |
| between Tier 3 and Tier 4 - Adaptive | Level 4 Managed | Measurements per subcategory are quantified and tracked as management metrics |
| Tier 4 - Adaptive | Level 5 Optimizing | The organisation improves the subcategory practice itself and contributes it back |

## Integration with the other mapped standards

- **NIST AI RMF + ISO/IEC 42001:2023** - the GOVERN function carries most of the AIMS clause obligations (clauses 5, 6 and 7), MAP carries clause 4 context work, MEASURE carries clause 9 performance evaluation, and MANAGE carries clauses 8 and 10. See [ISO 42001 Mapping](AIMF-ISO42001-MAPPING.md), which draws on the same catalog column `ISO42001_Clauses`.
- **NIST AI RMF + ISO/IEC 27001:2022** - the secure-and-resilient and privacy-enhanced characteristics are carried by the information-security controls attributed in [ISO 27001 Mapping](AIMF-ISO27001-MAPPING.md); the AI-specific subcategories (bias, explainability, environmental impact) have no ISMS analogue and are flagged there.
- **NIST AI RMF + EO 14179** - the RMF is the measurement instrument federal agencies are directed to use. [EO 14179 Mapping](AIMF-EXECUTIVE-ORDER-14179-MAPPING.md) records which AIMF controls make an organisation ready for the federal expectations that followed the order.
- **Function-level maturity views** - [GOVERN](GOVERN-FUNCTION-MATURITY-MAPPING.md), [MAP](MAP-FUNCTION-MATURITY-MAPPING.md), [MEASURE](MEASURE-FUNCTION-MATURITY-MAPPING.md) and [MANAGE](MANAGE-FUNCTION-MATURITY-MAPPING.md) maturity mappings expand each function.

## Maintenance

This document is generated, not hand-maintained. To change it, change `core/AIMF-CONTROL-CATALOG.csv` and re-run:

```
python3 scripts/aimf_mappings.py generate    # re-render every generated block
python3 scripts/aimf_mappings.py check       # fail on drift, placeholders or bad references
```

`check` fails if the rendered tables on disk differ from the catalog, if a control id referenced in any mapping document is absent from the catalog, or if an ISO 27001 reference uses pre-2022 numbering. A mapping document therefore cannot claim coverage the catalog does not have.
