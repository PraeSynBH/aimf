## Executive Order Overview

Executive Order 14179 represents the U.S. government's comprehensive strategy for managing AI risks while promoting innovation. It establishes requirements for federal AI use and sets standards for AI development.

### Core Principles

1. **Safety and Security**: AI systems must be safe and secure
2. **Innovation and Competition**: Promote AI innovation in the U.S.
3. **Support for Workers**: Address AI's impact on the workforce
4. **Equity and Civil Rights**: Advance equity and civil rights
5. **Consumer Protection**: Protect consumers from AI harms
6. **Privacy and Civil Liberties**: Strengthen privacy protections
7. **Federal AI Use**: Manage AI use by federal government
8. **International Leadership**: Promote responsible AI globally
