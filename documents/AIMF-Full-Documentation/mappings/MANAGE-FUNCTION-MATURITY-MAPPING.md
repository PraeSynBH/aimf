## Overview

The MANAGE function allocates risk resources to mapped and measured risks on a regular basis. It translates risk understanding into action through prioritization, response planning, and continuous risk treatment.

**ISO 42001 Primary Mapping**:
- MANAGE aligns primarily with ISO 42001 **Clause 8 (Operation)**, specifically:
  - 8.1 Operational planning and control
  - 8.2 AI system impact assessment
- Also connects to **Clause 10 (Improvement)**:
  - 10.1 Nonconformity and corrective action
  - 10.2 Continual improvement
- And **Clause 6.1** (Actions to address risks and opportunities)

**MANAGE Function Structure**:
- **4 Categories**: Risk Prioritization/Response, Benefits/Impact Strategies, Third-Party Risk, Treatment/Recovery/Communication
- **10 Subcategories**: Risk management execution requirements
- **Cross-cutting**: Informed by MAP and MEASURE, implements GOVERN policies

## Level 1: Initial - No Systematic AI Risk Management

**ISO 42001 Reference**: Gaps in Clauses 6.1, 8, and 10 - No systematic risk management, operational control, or improvement

### Overview
Organizations at Level 1 have no structured approach to managing AI risks. Risk responses are ad-hoc or absent, and there is no systematic resource allocation to address identified AI risks.

---

### MANAGE 1: AI Risks Prioritized, Responded To, and Managed

#### MANAGE 1.1: Determination on Whether to Proceed
- **Status**: No formal determination process
- **Characteristics**:
  - AI systems deployed without evaluating if they achieve intended purposes
  - No assessment of whether stated objectives are met
  - No formal go/no-go decision process
  - Development and deployment decisions made informally
  - No criteria for proceeding with AI systems

#### MANAGE 1.2: Risk Treatment Prioritization
- **Status**: Not prioritized
- **Characteristics**:
  - Documented AI risks not prioritized
  - Impact and likelihood not considered in resource allocation
  - Available resources not assessed
  - Risk treatment methods not evaluated
  - No prioritization framework

#### MANAGE 1.3: Risk Response Development
- **Status**: Not developed or planned
- **Characteristics**:
  - No responses developed for high priority risks
  - Risk response options not evaluated (mitigate, transfer, avoid, accept)
  - Risk responses not planned or documented
  - No risk treatment plans

#### MANAGE 1.4: Negative Residual Risks Documentation
- **Status**: Not documented
- **Characteristics**:
  - Residual risks not identified
  - Downstream impacts on acquirers/end users not documented
  - Total unmitigated risk not calculated
  - No residual risk communication

---

### MANAGE 2: Strategies to Maximize Benefits and Minimize Negative Impacts

#### MANAGE 2.1: Resources and Alternative Approaches
- **Status**: Not considered
- **Characteristics**:
  - Resources required for risk management not assessed
  - Non-AI alternatives not evaluated
  - Alternative methods not considered
  - Risk reduction strategies not explored
  - No comparison of approaches

#### MANAGE 2.2: Mechanisms to Sustain Value
- **Status**: Not in place
- **Characteristics**:
  - No mechanisms to sustain AI system value post-deployment
  - Value degradation not monitored
  - No maintenance strategy for value preservation
  - System performance not actively sustained

#### MANAGE 2.3: Procedures for Unknown Risks
- **Status**: Not established
- **Characteristics**:
  - No procedures to respond to previously unknown risks
  - No process for handling emergent risks
  - Recovery procedures not defined
  - Unknown risk response ad-hoc

#### MANAGE 2.4: Mechanisms to Supersede/Disengage/Deactivate
- **Status**: Not in place
- **Characteristics**:
  - No mechanisms to supersede AI systems
  - Disengagement procedures not defined
  - Deactivation processes not established
  - Responsibilities not assigned for system shutdown
  - Performance inconsistencies not addressed systematically

---

## Level 3: Defined - Standardized AI Risk Management Processes

**ISO 42001 Reference**: Substantial implementation of Clauses 6.1, 8, and 10 - Systematic risk management, operational control, and improvement in place

### Overview
Organizations at Level 3 have comprehensive, standardized processes for managing AI risks. All AI systems have documented risk management plans, and risk responses are systematically implemented and monitored.

---

### MANAGE 1: AI Risks Prioritized, Responded To, and Managed

#### MANAGE 1.1: Determination on Whether to Proceed
- **Status**: Formal, systematic process
- **Characteristics**:
  - All AI systems formally evaluated for intended purposes
  - Systematic assessment of objective achievement required
  - Formal go/no-go decision process with governance approval
  - All development and deployment decisions thoroughly documented
  - Clear, documented criteria for proceeding
  - Decision rationale maintained in records

#### MANAGE 1.2: Risk Treatment Prioritization
- **Status**: Comprehensively prioritized
- **Characteristics**:
  - All documented AI risks systematically prioritized
  - Impact and likelihood quantitatively assessed
  - Available resources comprehensively evaluated
  - All risk treatment methods systematically considered
  - Comprehensive prioritization framework applied consistently
  - Prioritization reviewed and updated regularly

#### MANAGE 1.3: Risk Response Development
- **Status**: Comprehensively developed and documented
- **Characteristics**:
  - Responses developed for all high priority risks
  - All risk response options systematically evaluated (mitigate, transfer, avoid, accept)
  - Comprehensive risk response plans documented
  - Treatment plans for all significant risks
  - Response effectiveness criteria defined
  - Response owners assigned

#### MANAGE 1.4: Negative Residual Risks Documentation
- **Status**: Comprehensively documented
- **Characteristics**:
  - All residual risks systematically identified and quantified
  - All downstream impacts on acquirers/end users documented
  - Total unmitigated risk calculated and reported
  - Comprehensive residual risk communication to all stakeholders
  - Residual risk acceptance formally documented
